# 🙌 Friends Hope Helping Hands Foundation

**Registered NGO | Reg. No: 1133/2018 | Active since 2016**

> *"Anybody can do social service even with ₹200 per month."*

---

## 🕊️ Mission
To transform lives through **consistent compassion**, enabling **underprivileged individuals** and **vulnerable communities** to lead a dignified life by providing **food**, **education**, **healthcare**, and **emergency relief**.

---

## 🌟 Vision
To build a **selfless society** where *every rupee donated brings hope*, *every hand helps*, and *every action matters* — ultimately creating **10,000 active donors** committed to monthly support.

---

## 📌 Campaign Goals (May–July 2025)
- 🎯 Reach **10,000 monthly ₹200 subscribers** via Razorpay auto-debit
- 🛒 Enable 53+ events under #HopeOnWheels, #HopeEducationSupport, and #HopeMedicalRelief
- 🤝 Empower local volunteers across both **Telugu states**
- 📢 Launch a multilingual digital campaign (English + Telugu)
